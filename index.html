Simple Cop OT Calculator Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

This policy explains how Simple Cop OT Calculator ("the app") handles your information. The short version: we do not collect your data. What you enter stays on your own device. If you use the web version while signed in to Claude, a private copy is also kept with your Claude account so your entries are not lost (see "Web version backup copy" below).

What the app stores

When you use the app, it saves the following on your device only:

  • Dates you log and the times you worked
  • Overtime hours the app calculates from those times
  • Your regular tour start and end times
  • Your chosen squad, used to show your days off
  • Your backup text, only if you create one
  • Your answers to the next-day and travel time questions
  • Your reminder settings, only if you turn reminders on

In the App Store version, this information is saved inside the app on your phone. In the web version, it is saved in your browser's storage on the device you use. In both versions, it is not sent to us.

Web version backup copy

If you use the web version of the app while signed in to Claude, the app also keeps a private copy of your entries, regular tour and squad with your Claude account. This lets your entries come back if your browser clears its storage. Only you can see this copy; we cannot read it. It is stored by Anthropic, the company that runs Claude, under Anthropic's own privacy policy. The App Store version does not use this copy.

Reminders

If you turn on the payday reminder, the app schedules notifications on your phone using your phone's built-in notification system. The reminder text, including the overtime you logged for that pay period, is created on your phone and is not sent to us. You can turn reminders off at any time in the Reminders section or in your phone's Settings.

What we do not collect

  • We do not require an account, a name, an email address, a badge or tax ID number, or any employer information.
  • We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or device identifiers.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics, or tracking tools in the app.
  • We do not sell, rent, or share your information, because we do not have it.

Backups

The Backup and restore feature creates a block of text containing your saved entries. It appears on your screen and is copied to your clipboard. Where you paste or store that text, such as in a note or an email to yourself, is your choice. We do not receive it. Anyone who has the backup text can read your entries, so keep it somewhere private.

Deleting your data

You can delete everything the app has saved at any time using the "Delete all my data from this device" button in the Backup and restore section. In the web version, this also deletes the private copy kept with your Claude account. This cannot be undone unless you made a backup first. Deleting the app, or clearing your browser's site data in the web version, also removes the entries saved on that device. Without a backup, deleted entries cannot be recovered.

Hosting

The App Store version is distributed by Apple, and the web version is hosted by Anthropic through Claude. Apple and Anthropic may record basic technical information, such as your device type, IP address or app downloads, under their own privacy policies. This is separate from the entries you save in the app, which we never receive.

Children

The app is intended for working adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Accuracy and affiliation

The app is a personal tracking tool. Pay dates, pay periods, and day-off calendars shown in the app are for convenience and may contain errors. Always confirm against your official pay and schedule records. The app is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any police department, city agency, employer, or union.

Security

Because your information stays on your device, its security depends on your device. Use a screen lock and keep your phone and browser up to date.

Changes to this policy

If the app changes in a way that affects your privacy, such as adding accounts or cloud sync, we will update this policy and change the effective date above before those features are released. The policy will describe what is collected, why, and how to delete it.
